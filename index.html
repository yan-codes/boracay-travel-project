Why Boracay?

Boracay, a world-famous tropical island in the Philippines

Boracay, the most iconic island in the Philipines, is popular for its beaches and blessed with a long stretch of powdery white sand, crystal clear and azure waters, and a stunning sunset. Boracay has won multiple awards in previous years. In 2019, Boracay was the top of the Asia Category in Conde Nast Traveler's The Best Islands in the World 2019 Readers Choice Awards. In 2020, Boracay retains its Top 10 island status alongside other Philippine Islands as it places 6th in the Top 10 list.

Restaurants

My favorite places to eat in Boracay

Aquafresh Seafood and Chicken Inasal

The original seafood restuarant in Aklan since 1994. Catering to "cook-to-order" service, ala carte and big group reservations up to 50 persons.

Address:

Beachfront, Station 2, Boracay Island, Malay, 5608 Aklan

What I like about it

Serves fresh seafood right on Station 2 Beach Front, with oysters, grilled fish, and daily seafood restocks. Reasonably priced too!

Learn more

The Lazy Dog Bed & Breakfast

A rustic, pet-friendly lodge located just a 1-minute walk from Bulabog Beach in Balabag, Boracay. It features a leafy courtyard and a garden cafe wrapped around a century-old evergreen tree.

Address:

1 Sesame Street, Bulabog Beach, Balabag, Boracay, Malay, 5608 Aklan

What I like about it

The place serves home-cooked local and international comfort food, coffee, craft beer, and local ginger kombucha. Pet dogs are welcome. 🐶

Learn more

The Hobbit Tavern

A one-of-a-kind island dining experience! Enjoy delicious food, cold drinks, and warm hospitality from the world's friendliest little servers.

Address:

D'mall de Boracay, Malay, Aklan

What I like about it

Run by real-life hobbits, this tavern offers both local and international cuisine. You can also view replicas of classic paintings displayed throughout the restaurant, all created by the owner, who is an artist.
